60-Second Interview Elevator Pitch

"Hello, I am a Business Analyst from Uncodemy. I designed and deployed an end-to-end Student Performance & Placement Analytics System for a tech career acceleration academy enrolling 1,000 students across 6 domains. The institute faced a 14.2% mid-cohort dropout bottleneck and stagnant placement conversions. I modeled a 7-table 3NF MySQL relational database, engineered 21 analytical SQL queries with window functions, constructed a Power BI Kimball Star Schema with 30+ DAX measures, and developed this connected full-stack web application. My analysis revealed a critical retention chasm at Module 5, proved that attendance >85% drives ₹2.4 LPA higher CTC, and delivered an Early Warning Counselor Alert Queue projected to protect ₹18.4 Lakhs in annual tuition."

Relational Database (3NF)
  • 7 Tables: courses, trainers, batches, students, attendance, assessments, placements
  • Foreign key cascading and check constraints
  • 21 Production BA SQL queries with Window Functions

  • Formulas: `XLOOKUP`, `IFS`, `SUMIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`
  • Dynamic arrays (`FILTER`, `SORT`) & Pivot tables
Power BI Star Schema
  • Kimball Star Schema with 1-to-many filters
  • 30+ Production DAX Measures (Employability Index)
